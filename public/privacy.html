Privacy and terms

Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

Your lore stays on your device. GOONOMICS has no server side processing of your answers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies.

What happens to what you type

  • Every calculation runs in your browser. Your ages and numbers are never sent anywhere.
  • Answers live in memory and are gone when you close or reload the tab.
  • The only thing stored is your 18+ confirmation, in this tab's session storage.
  • Share images and copied text are generated in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Friend comparison uses only a score you type in yourself. It is not saved or sent.

Hosting

The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, it receives standard request data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. It never receives your answers.

Images

Mascot and illustration art was generated once, before launch. Your inputs are never sent to any image service.

Adults only

GOONOMICS is a comedy calculator about self reported masturbation statistics for people aged 18 and over. It contains no explicit imagery. We do not verify identity; the age screen is a self declaration.

Not medical advice

Results are entertainment estimates built from your own guesses and published averages. They are not a diagnosis and say nothing about your health, fertility or worth. If anything ever feels out of control, talk to a professional.

Terms

Use GOONOMICS for fun, share your own results however you like, and do not use it to harass anyone. The site is provided as is, without warranties.
